# A spoken Review, Stage 2: Review as a pass held on the server — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Review becomes a pass held on the server: one ordered list with one cursor every device and (in Stage 3) the connector move; Next leaves an item undecided and writes nothing to the task, Back returns, Next after the last comes round to what was left; every decision on Review goes through `review_decide.plan`; and Review orders by urgency, shows who wrote behind "Thread updated" and "Mentioned in Slack", recommends an answer where the data supports one, groups alike new tasks, and can say each item in words for the ear.

**Architecture:** `factotum/review_pass.py` holds the order and the cursor (`review_passes`, `review_pass_items`), never what an item says (built from `review_queue.items` on every read) and never what was decided (the tray's `decides` column). Every pass write starts with `db.begin_write` and is compare-and-set on `rev`. The page's Review becomes one controller (`rp`, `reviewCurrent`, `reviewOptions`, `reviewRun`) over the pass's View; every decision is `decideOn` → `POST /api/review/decide`. Recommendations (`factotum/recommend.py`), groups (in `review_pass`) and the spoken card (`factotum/spoken.py`) are data on the View.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, FastAPI, SQLite 3.45 (WAL, legacy transaction control), pytest offline; one `app.js` with no build step; node for the tray-mirror tests; playwright-core for the rendered checks.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-spoken-review-design.md`, sections 1, 2, 4 (the contract: its names exactly), 6 (this stage), 9 to 12. This plan does not repeat the design's tables, signatures or test names; it adds what a reading of the code at `f3aca82` gives. The design's line numbers are for `75d2c77`; the ones this plan gives are for `f3aca82` and are the ones to use.

Keep the name; add keyword-only `cfg=None, today=None` (Deviation D1); without them it only sets `done_batch_id`.

## Deviations from the design

- **D1. `after_apply(conn, decided, batch_id, *, cfg=None, today=None)`.** The contract's three arguments cannot tell whether an Apply left nothing undecided (Trap 1). The name and the first three arguments are the contract's; `tray.apply` passes the two keywords.
- **D2. Design commit 2a-2 is two commits, 2a-2a and 2a-2b.** 2a-2a moves every Review decision onto `decideOn` on the page as it is and fixes the mirror's four faults (notes 1, 2, 3, 7); 2a-2b puts the page on the pass. Why: the parity check compares exactly one change (who stages a press) and the rest of 2a-2 would hide a difference in a redraw; and 2a-2 as one commit is the largest unit of the build, which a builder who may be stopped at any moment should not hold uncommitted. 2a-2a is deployable by itself (Stage 1's page with every decision through the rule).
- **D3. A decision on an open task outside the queue still stages, without a move** (Trap 3, Review Focus 4). The design's `Gone` would undo Stage 1's deliberate rule that a card conversation's decision works after the reason went. `Gone` stays for what `load_item` refuses.
- **D4. The View carries `ahead`**: the entry a forward move or a decision on the item in front would bring to the front, in `current`'s shape, or null. The page draws it at the click and the answer replaces it (Trap 4, Review Focus 1). Additive; Stage 3 may ignore it.
- **D5. `reviewShown` moves in 2a-2b**, not 2a-3: the `review` object it reads is removed there (Trap 5). 2a-3 keeps following and `onReturn`.
- **D6. `review_pass.decide` checks only the pass-only options (`rec`, `keep_all`) against `options()`; the rule refuses every other option itself.** One list of what an item accepts (`review_decide.OPTIONS` and `plan`'s checks) instead of two that could drift. The answer is the same `Refused` (400).
- **D7. Design commit 2f is two commits: 2f-1 closes the first half, 2f-2 the second.** The first half is deployed before the second is built, and CLAUDE.md's conventions on `GET /api/review` and the stamp become false at 2a-3.
- **D8. Task 0** (a test-only commit, reviewer note 9) comes before 2b-1.

- The deploy order is the lead's: the first half (through 2f-1) together, with a backup-API copy of the live database first and the release note; the second half after it. Never deploy 2a-3 without 2a-2b (Trap 10).
