## Task: draft a reply

Find the thread for the attached task (counterpart address first), read its latest messages, and save
a reply draft on that thread with `mail_draft_reply`: short, plain, in the first person, answering
what was asked; no signature (Mailbird adds it). Then say in one line what the draft says and where it
is. Propose `link_thread` for the thread and the change the reply implies (usually `waiting_on` the
other person from today, or `done` when the reply closes the matter). If you cannot find the thread,
say so and propose nothing.
